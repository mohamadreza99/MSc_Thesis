\centerline{\textbf{\large{Abstract}}}
\begin{quote}
	
Few-shot learning methods have achieved notable performance in recent years.
However, few-shot learning in large-scale settings with hundreds of classes is still
challenging. In this dissertation, we tackle the problems of large-scale few-shot learning
by taking advantage of pre-trained foundation models. We recast the original
problem in two levels with different granularity.

At the coarse-grained level, we
introduce a novel object recognition approach with robustness to sub-population
shifts. At the fine-grained level, generative experts are designed for few-shot
learning, specialized for different superclasses. 
A Bayesian schema is considered
to combine coarse-grained information with fine-grained predictions in a winnertakes-all fashion. 

Extensive experiments on large-scale datasets and different
architectures show that the proposed method is both effective and efficient besides
its simplicity and natural problem remodeling.


\vskip 1cm
\textbf{Keywords:} \textit{Deep Learning, Large-Scale Classification, Few-Shot Learning, Sub-Population Shift, Hierarchical Classification, Generalization, Robustness, Bayesian Learning}
\end{quote}
